# Draft: request to an adjudicator

Send this to an accounting lecturer or a senior accounting student. Edit the greeting and sign-off before sending.

---

**Subject:** Could you help settle accounting questions in our research benchmark?

Dear [Name],

We are three final-year students building FaithGuard, a system that checks AI answers about company financial reports and fixes or blocks wrong numbers. Part of the project is a benchmark of US and Sri Lankan annual-report questions, labelled by hand.

When two of us label the same answer differently, or when an accounting point is genuinely unclear (for example, Group versus Company figures, restated comparatives, or which profit measure a question means), we need an independent expert to settle it. We would be very grateful if you could act as that adjudicator.

**What it would involve**

- Reviewing a short list of disputed items, each showing the question, the report extract, the answer and our two labels.
- Choosing the correct label or marking the item "ambiguous", with a one-line reason.
- We expect this to take a few hours in total, spread over two or three sessions. We will know the size better after our pilot and will tell you before you commit.

**What we can offer**

- Acknowledgement in every paper and report that uses the benchmark.
- A copy of the finished benchmark and papers.

You would never label anything we can settle ourselves, and nothing needs to be done until our labelling phase.

Kind regards,
M.L Ahamed, M.S.A Ahamed and Sharaf
